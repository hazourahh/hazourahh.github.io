I am very much looking forward to chairing the 14th International Workshop on Quality in Databases (QDB25) at VLDB2025 together with Lisa Ehrlinger and Lorena Etcheverry this Monday.

For QDB25, we have put together a strong and diverse program featuring research paper presentations and inspiring keynotes by Renée J. Miller  and Paolo Missier. We also have a poster session this year to add interactivity and a space for discussions. 


See program details on our <a href='https://qdb-workshop.github.io/'> website</a> 

I look forward to many stimulating discussions on data quality, data-centric AI, and the future of trustworthy data systems at QDB25!
